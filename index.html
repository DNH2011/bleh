Welcome, planeswalker.

Build your battlefield, manage your mana, and outplay an AI opponent. Each player gets a 250-card deck built around two or three colors.

How to play: play lands from your hand, tap them for mana, cast spells, then attack with creatures on later turns. Targeting spells enter a visible spell area: click that spell, then click a legal target.

Rules & controls

Mana: click an untapped land to tap it. The game pays a card's colored and generic costs automatically. Color letters are W (white), B (blue), K (black), R (red), G (green).

Targets: cast a targeted spell, click its card in the spell area, then click a legal permanent or player. The game rejects illegal targets. Click Cancel target to clear the selection.

Decks: both decks contain 250 cards, use a focused two/three-color identity, matching basic lands and a few colorless artifacts. The AI picks legal targets automatically.
