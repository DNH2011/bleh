Combat

Choose only the creatures you want to attack with, then confirm. During defense, click an AI attacker, then one of your untapped creatures to assign it as a blocker. You may leave any attacker unblocked. You never need to block with every creature.

Mana

Colored mana can pay generic mana costs. For a 3WW cost, pay any five mana sources, of which at least two must be white. Colored mana requirements cannot be paid by wrong colors.

Catalog

Welcome, planeswalker.

Both players have 250-card decks built around two or three matching colors. The game includes classic cards plus Arabian Nights cards. You choose individual attackers and blockers; no creature is required to enter combat.

How combat works

Click Choose attackers, select your ready creatures, then confirm. When the AI attacks, click each attacking creature you want to block and then click one of your untapped creatures. Confirm to finish defense. AI creatures can attack or block only with some of their available creatures.
