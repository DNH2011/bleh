Battle log

Using activated abilities

Click one of your battlefield cards to select it. Any activated abilities it has appear as buttons in the ability panel. Click an ability, pay its cost, then choose a highlighted legal target if required. To cast a targeted spell, click the spell card on the stack prompt, then click a legal target.

Deck & mana

Both players use 250-card, two/three-color decks. Any colored mana can pay generic costs; colored symbols still require the matching mana.

Card catalog

Welcome, planeswalker.

Both players draw from focused-color decks. Click a permanent to reveal its activated abilities; ability buttons let you pay costs and select targets. Targeted spells and abilities use the same highlighted-target flow.

How to play

Click a card on your battlefield to select it. Activated abilities show as buttons with costs and rules text. Choose an ability, pay its mana/tap cost, then click a highlighted legal target. To target with a spell, play it, click its pending spell card, then choose a legal permanent or player. Click a card in hand to cast it; available mana is paid automatically.
