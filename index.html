How combat works

Click Choose attackers, then select any ready creatures you want to attack with. Confirm when ready—your other creatures stay back. When AI attacks, click an attacking creature, then one of your untapped creatures to assign it as a blocker. Assign only the blockers you want and confirm; any unblocked attacker deals damage to you. You can leave any attacker unblocked.

Decks

Both players use 250-card decks with a randomly selected two- or three-color identity and matching lands. Cards display their name, cost, illustration, rules, and creature stats.

Welcome, planeswalker.

Choose attackers and blockers freely: you are never required to attack or block with every creature. Assign blocks individually during the AI's combat.

Rules & controls

Play lands and tap them for mana. Choose Choose attackers to enter attacker selection; click creatures to toggle them, then confirm. During opponent attacks, click an attacker followed by a legal untapped blocker. Confirm blocks to resolve combat. Creatures can attack on a turn after they entered play.
