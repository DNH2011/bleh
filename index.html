Battle log

Priority & instants

You may cast instant spells during your own turn, during the opponent's response window, and while assigning blockers. During the AI turn, click Pass priority to let its actions continue. Targeted instants use the same click-spell-then-target flow.

Activated abilities

Click one of your battlefield cards; available ability buttons appear below it. Activate the ability, pay its cost, then choose its highlighted target where required.

Deck & mana

Each player has a 250-card, two/three-color deck. Any colored mana can pay generic costs; colored symbols require the matching mana.

Card catalog

Welcome, planeswalker.

Both players draw from focused-color decks. Instants can be played during either player's turn when you have priority. Graveyards are shown below the hand.

How to play

Click a card on your battlefield to see activated abilities. Click Choose attackers, select ready creatures, then confirm. During AI attacks, select attacker, then a blocker. To cast an instant at any time, click it while you have priority. During the opponent turn, click Pass priority when you're done responding. Both graveyards are expandable below your hand.
