Game guide

Play one land per turn. Tap lands to make mana, then cast cards from your hand. Creatures can attack starting the turn after they enter play. Click your creatures to select them, then attack. At 0 life you lose.

Welcome, planeswalker.

Build your battlefield, manage your mana, and outplay an AI opponent. This prototype turns a playable selection of the cards you listed into a simple head-to-head duel. Classic card text is adapted where needed for a quick, playable game.

How to play: play lands from your hand, tap them for mana, cast spells, then attack with creatures on later turns. The AI plays automatically when you end your turn.

Rules & controls

Mana: click an untapped land to tap it for mana; click it again to untap during your turn. The game pays generic costs automatically from available mana. Color letters are W (white), B (blue), K (black), R (red), G (green).

Play: click a card in hand to cast it if you can pay. Creatures enter tapped for summoning sickness and can attack next turn. Use Attack to send ready creatures; the opponent's creatures may block.

Spells: this demo implements core effects for its included playable selection. Other text is shown on the card; unimplemented specialty effects are informational.
