Targets & abilities

Cast a targeted spell or activate an ability, then click its card in the pending-spell area and click a legal target. Click one of your battlefield cards to select it; the ability panel shows available actions. Target abilities spend their cost only when you activate them. The game checks legal target type and controller where applicable.

Mana

Click lands and mana artifacts to tap/untap them. You can instead click a playable non-land in hand; the game automatically taps matching sources. Any color can pay generic cost, but colored symbols require their matching color.

Welcome, planeswalker.

Both players draw from 250-card, focused-color decks. Click a permanent and use its ability button to activate it. To target something, click the pending spell or ability, then click the legal creature/permanent.

Targeting & activated abilities

Click a card in hand to cast it. If it says “target”, it appears in the pending area; click the spell, then a legal target. Click a permanent you control to open its actions. Abilities with targets enter the same target-selection flow; costs such as tapping the creature or paying mana are charged when you activate them.
